## About Agenda

Agenda is a journal and planner built around one simple idea: notes should live on dates. Instead of scattering thoughts across loose pages and separate task lists, Agenda journal planner keeps each entry anchored to the day it belongs to. Open Agenda, pick a date, and write. The structure is already there.

This matters for people who think in days. Students tracking assignments, professionals logging meetings, writers keeping a daily record, and anyone who wants a readable timeline of their own thinking. Agenda date notes stay connected to the calendar, so a note written on Monday is still findable on Monday weeks later. Agenda diary schedule views show what was planned and what actually happened, side by side on the same page.

Agenda is not trying to be everything. It is a focused environment for dated writing and planning. Agenda calendar entries, daily reflections, and forward-looking plans all share the same home. There is no hunting through folders, no guessing which file holds which day. The date is the address, and Agenda respects that.

For longer use, Agenda grows quietly with you. A week of entries becomes a month, a month becomes a season, and the journal stays navigable because every note already knows its place. Agenda daily planning sessions feel less like administration and more like routine, which is exactly what a journal should be.
